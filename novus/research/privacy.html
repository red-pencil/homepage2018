VR Privacy: Privacy Notifications in Immersive Virtual Environments

VR Privacy：沉浸式虚拟现实环境中的隐私通知设计

This project investigated privacy awareness and notification design in multi-user virtual reality (VR) environments. We explored the design space of privacy notifications, examining how privacy-related information could be communicated to users within immersive experiences. We designed and implemented an interactive multi-user VR prototype using Unity and Oculus headsets to demonstrate different notification approaches. User studies were conducted to evaluate participants' understanding, perceptions, and responses to privacy notifications, considering factors such as usability, awareness, and potential interruptions to immersion. The research aimed to identify design considerations for effective privacy notifications and contribute to the development of more privacy-aware social VR systems.

本项目研究多人虚拟现实（VR）环境中的隐私意识与通知机制设计，探索如何在沉浸式交互过程中有效地向用户传达隐私相关信息。研究首先对 VR 隐私通知的设计空间进行探索与分析，考察不同通知方式在虚拟环境中的应用。随后，使用 Unity 和 Oculus VR 头显开发了支持多用户交互的实验原型，实现并展示不同的隐私通知方案。通过用户实验，评估参与者对隐私通知的理解、感知及行为反应，重点考察隐私意识、可用性以及通知对沉浸体验的潜在干扰。研究旨在为社交 VR 系统中的隐私通知与隐私保护交互设计提供参考。
