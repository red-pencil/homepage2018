AIGC for ASD: Personalized Adaptive Social Storytelling

AIGC for ASD：面向孤独症儿童的个性化自适应社交故事系统

This project explores the use of AI-generated content (AIGC) to support personalized learning and social-emotional development for children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD). We developed an adaptive interactive storytelling system that generates individualized social stories based on children's characteristics, learning goals, and behavioral responses. The system dynamically adjusts story content during interactions and across learning sessions by tracking children's progress and evolving needs. The research involved classroom observations, controlled experiments, pre- and post-tests, and interviews. Quantitative and qualitative methods, including mixed-design ANOVA and thematic analysis, were used to evaluate children's learning experiences and the potential of AI-supported personalized education.

本项目探索利用人工智能生成内容（AIGC），支持孤独症谱系障碍（ASD）儿童的个性化学习与社会情感能力发展。研究开发了一套自适应交互式故事系统，根据儿童的个体特征、学习目标和行为反馈，生成个性化社交故事。系统不仅能够在交互过程中实时调整故事内容，还能够持续追踪儿童的学习进度与需求变化，实现跨学习阶段的动态个性化。研究采用课堂观察、控制实验、前后测及访谈等方法，并结合混合设计方差分析（Mixed-design ANOVA）和主题分析（Thematic Analysis），评估儿童的学习体验及人工智能辅助个性化教育的应用潜力。
