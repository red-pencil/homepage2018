### P1 — Visuomotor Influence of Attached Robotic Neck Augmentation

Lichao Shen, MHD Yamen Saraiji, Kai Kunze, Kouta Minamizawa, and Roshan Lalintha Peiris. **2020.** *Visuomotor Influence of Attached Robotic Neck Augmentation*. In **ACM Symposium on Spatial User Interaction (SUI '20)**, Article 14, 1–10.  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3385959.3418460  
**Lichao's position:** First author. **Format:** Full conference paper.

**Research evidence:** A wearable, three-degree-of-freedom robotic neck and stereo-vision interface that alters the mapping between head/neck movement and the direction of perceived vision. The paper describes prototype implementation and human-participant evaluations of visual scanning and hand-involved performance. In the tested conditions, the paper reports approximately **22% less response time** and **28% faster response speed** in overall scanning measures. These are study-specific reported results, not universal guarantees.

**Why it matters:** This is direct research evidence spanning a physical interactive apparatus, programmable sensorimotor mapping, human-centered experimental evaluation, and academic publication. The CV states that Lichao led the related project; the first-author position and paper independently establish authorship, not every specific division of labour.
